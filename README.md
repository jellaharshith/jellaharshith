Hey! I’m Harshith Jella, a Computer Science student at California State University, East Bay, driven by a deep passion for Cybersecurity, ethical hacking, and technology innovation.

I’m fascinated by how systems communicate, how they fail, and — most importantly — how they can be secured and optimized. My curiosity has led me to explore both offensive and defensive security, learning how attackers think and how defenders respond. I believe this dual perspective builds stronger, smarter systems.

I’m constantly expanding my skill set — from penetration testing and malware analysis to network defense, scripting, and automation. Beyond tools and tech, I’m motivated by the challenge of solving real-world problems that protect people, data, and organizations.

Outside the screen, I’m someone who enjoys exploring the unknown — whether it’s new technologies, cybersecurity labs, or creative ideas connecting AI and security. I thrive on learning, experimenting, and collaborating with others who share a passion for making digital spaces safer.

✨ Curious mind. Security mindset. Always learning, always evolving. 🔐
